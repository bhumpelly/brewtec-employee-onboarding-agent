Limitations

  • Nothing here is measured in a real company. BrewTec is fictional. The volume, the 6 hours per hire and the 1-in-3 access problem are scenario figures from the project scope.
  • The 60 minutes after automation is a target, not a measurement. The time sheet test_pack/time_saved_hires.csv for 5 hires is still empty. Timing the 5 hires replaces the largest uncertainty on the automated side.
  • Rework is left out, not set to zero. A hire without working access costs a lost day and rushed IT work, but there is no data on how much. Including it would raise the savings, so leaving it out is the cautious choice.
  • Assistant time savings are not counted. People Ops answering fewer policy questions is real but unmeasured. Hand-offs to #hr-helpdesk are counted inside the 60 minutes.
  • Hosting is zero only because the build runs on a laptop. Production in Germany (for example Hetzner, about 5 to 10 EUR a month) and a paid AI tier would add cost. Enter them to see the effect.
  • AI cost is in USD and converted at a stated 0.90. Token counts per question are estimates. LangSmith traces show the real counts. At these volumes the AI cost is under 1 EUR a month and does not change the result.
  • Build hours are an estimate of the capstone effort at the same labour rate. A company buying this from a contractor would pay a higher rate.
